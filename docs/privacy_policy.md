# Spokefolio Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026 — **Draft v0.1, prepared for legal review before publication**

Spokefolio is a career knowledge base and resume tailoring service operated by
XFH Game Studio ("we", "us", "our"), Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. This policy
explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices
you have. It applies to the Spokefolio application and website.

**Questions or requests:** KellarM@xfhgamestudioltd.com — 780-504-4899

---

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address, and password when you
create an account. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.

**Career information you provide.** Spokefolio is designed around your career
history. Depending on how you use it, this may include:

- Profile details: name, phone, location, LinkedIn or website links, and a
  headline you write
- Employment history: job titles, employers, dates, locations,
  accomplishments, skills, metrics, and summaries
- Education history
- Documents you upload (for example, an old resume, transcript, certificate,
  or performance review)
- Job postings you save or paste, including employer names and descriptions
- Workplace addresses you enter so we can estimate your commute

**Voice recordings.** If you use voice intake, we receive an audio recording
of what you say. The recording is used to produce a written transcript.
**The audio itself is deleted once transcription is complete; the transcript
is kept as part of your career entries.**

**Application content.** Resumes, cover letters, match summaries, and notes
generated or saved within the service.

**Usage information.** Basic technical logs (device type, browser, pages
used) needed to run and secure the service.

**Payment information.** We do not collect or store your credit card number.
Payments are processed by third-party payment processors; we receive only
confirmation of the transaction and, where needed, the amount paid.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

1. Provide the service — building your career knowledge base, generating
   resumes and cover letters, and tailoring content to job postings
2. Calculate commute estimates when you provide a workplace address
3. Communicate with you about your account
4. Maintain, secure, and improve the service
5. Meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third
parties for their own marketing.

## 3. Artificial intelligence and third-party processing

Spokefolio uses artificial intelligence services operated by third-party
providers to transcribe voice input, extract information from your documents,
and generate summaries, match analyses, and resume content. These providers
process your career information on our instructions, solely to deliver the
service to you. AI-generated content is a starting point — you should review
it for accuracy before relying on it.

Commute estimates use third-party mapping services, which receive the
addresses needed to calculate the route.

## 4. Who can see your information

Your career information belongs to your account. The service enforces
per-user data isolation: other users cannot see your profile, entries,
documents, or applications. We access your information only as needed to
operate, support, and secure the service.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

We use essential local storage and cookies to keep you signed in and remember
your preferences, and basic analytics to understand how the service is used.
You can clear local storage through your browser at any time; some features
require sign-in to work.

## 6. How long we keep your information

We keep your career information for as long as your account is active — that
is the point of the service. If you delete your account, we delete your
profile, career entries, uploaded documents, and application data within 30
days, except where retention is required by law. If you delete an individual
entry or application, it is removed from the service.

## 7. Your privacy rights

You may:

- **Access** the personal information in your account
- **Correct or update** it at any time
- **Export** your data (your resume and structured entries)
- **Delete** individual entries or your entire account
- **Withdraw consent** for voice processing by discontinuing voice intake

To exercise any of these, use the in-app controls or contact us at
KellarM@xfhgamestudioltd.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you
may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada
(www.priv.gc.ca) or the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta
(www.oipc.ab.ca).

## 8. Security

The service runs over encrypted connections (HTTPS), uses per-user data
isolation, and stores passwords as secure hashes. No system is perfectly
secure; we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of career
information and will notify affected users if a breach ever requires it.

## 9. Children

Spokefolio is intended for adults making career decisions. The service is not
directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their
personal information.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be
announced in the application, and the effective date above will be revised.
Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
