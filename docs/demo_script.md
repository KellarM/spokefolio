# Spokefolio — 60–90 Second Demo Script (v1.0)

**Format:** One continuous screen recording, 16:9. One take, real pace, no
music bed needed for v1. Burned-in captions (most people watch sound-off).
Crop a 9:16 version for Shorts/Reels later.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Spokefolio home, cursor idle, then move | "Every resume tool makes you start over — every form, every job. Spokefolio flips it." |
| 0:08–0:25 | Voice intake running. Speak naturally: "Operations supervisor, twelve years in distribution, cut dock turnaround by 30 percent..." | (your own voice intake is the audio) "Talk your way through your career. It interviews you like a good recruiter would." |
| 0:25–0:40 | Knowledge base view: structured entries, accomplishments, metrics, skills visible | "It doesn't just make a document. It builds your knowledge base — every role, every number, structured and reusable." |
| 0:40–0:60 | Paste a real job posting → roles list shows Recommended / Optional badges → page estimate reads "1 of 2" → resume assembles | "Paste any job posting. Spokefolio picks the roles that fit, leads with the matching experience, and keeps it to two pages." |
| 0:60–0:78 | Click Download PDF → show the finished two-page resume scrolling once | "Then export it. Free. PDF or Word — no subscription, no surprise charges to download your own resume." |
| 0:78–0:90 | Tagline card: "Tell it once. Tailored everywhere." + spokefolio.com | "Tell it once. Tailored everywhere. Spokefolio." |

**Recording rules:**
1. Use realistic data (not Lorem Ipsum) — viewers judge the tool by the sample career.
2. Cursor moves should be slow and deliberate; hover 1 second before clicking.
3. Zoom/crop on the tier badges and page estimate — those are the "wow" moments.
4. Speak the voice intake live; it doubles as proof the feature is real.
5. Keep the whole thing under 90 seconds; cut the middle if you must, never the hook or the export.

**Suggested on-screen text (burned captions):**
- Hook: "start over, every time" → "Spokefolio flips it"
- Tailor beat: "Only the roles that fit. Two pages. Always."
- Export beat: "Free PDF + Word export"
- End card: "Tell it once. Tailored everywhere." / "One flat price. No subscription."
